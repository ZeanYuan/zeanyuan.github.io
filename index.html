Introduction

Zean Yuan is a Ph.D. student supervised by Prof. Rui Chen at Chongqing University, Chongqing, China. He received his B.S. and M.S. degrees in mechanical engineering from Zhejiang University of Technology, Hangzhou, China (2018) and Chongqing University, Chongqing, China (2021), respectively, with Prof. Rui Chen as his master's supervisor. From 2025 to 2026, he was a visiting Ph.D. student in the Miniature Soft Robotics Lab led by Assoc. Prof. Guo Zhan Lum at Nanyang Technological University, Singapore. He was selected for the Young Talent Support Program for Ph.D. Students (2025–2026) by the China Association for Science and Technology (only 3,226 recipients nationwide).

His research interests include soft robotics, bioinspired robots, electrohydraulic actuators, and physical intelligence, especially high-dynamic, multifunctional, and reconfigurable soft robotic actuators/joints. To date, as first or co-first author, he has published six research papers in SCI journals such as Science Advances, Nature Communications (Featured Article), IEEE Transactions on Robotics, and Smart Materials and Structures. His co-first-authored paper in Nature Communications has been featured by The New York Times, The Straits Times, The Indian Express, and other media outlets. He serves as a reviewer for top robotics journals and conferences, including IEEE Transactions on Robotics (T-RO), IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters (RA-L), the IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems (IROS), and the IEEE-RAS International Conference on Humanoid Robots (Humanoids).

He expects to complete his Ph.D. in late 2026 and is currently seeking a postdoctoral position starting in early 2027, focusing on bioinspired soft robotics, electroactive polymer actuators, and embodied intelligence. Please feel free to contact him via email at yza@cqu.edu.cn if you have any suitable openings or relevant recommendations.
